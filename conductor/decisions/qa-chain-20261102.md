## Implementation and acceptance gates

Schema boot fixes, requirements persistence, scoped SQL authorization, confirmations,
Undo/Redo, unfinished worktree changes, full QA coverage, final verification and
CI/release delivery remain work items. They are not deferred product decisions.
Record concrete choices here if implementation reveals a genuine product fork;
use a reversible default where possible and state the consequence of changing it.

The collaboration repair must distinguish invitation acceptance from transport
access. A recipient-bound signed envelope does not make its underlying document
ticket nontransferable or expiring. Expiry governs accepting the invitation;
revoking an application role cannot erase bytes already received. A child-scoped
role does not make a whole-Verse replica confidential to that child. Issuer
authority, immutable owner/namespace identity and any required delegation proof
remain security gates, rather than product decisions that this ledger can waive.

Source investigation for D-QA-016 found no existing portable Owner proof in the
legacy hierarchy role rows. Their authentication comes from the replication seam.
The canonical capability adapter has different scopes and incomplete authority
history; it cannot be presented as an already implemented legacy-hierarchy proof.
The Owner API-token floor on the QA bridge is an administration gate, separate
from the actual entity issuer's Owner or verified delegated-Manager authority.
