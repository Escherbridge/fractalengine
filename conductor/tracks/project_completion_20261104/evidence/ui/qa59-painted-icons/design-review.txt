## Design and SDK evidence

Painting, size, color and the few used Button builders are centralized; existing callers retain their application state and callbacks. No icon-font, glyph or texture coverage dependency remains in these changed presentations.
- `egui::Ui::add_enabled` (`ui.rs:1848–1858`) invokes the widget under the disabled painter and restores the caller afterward; cloning that painter retains its opacity.
