   then same-root restart in J10 to distinguish persistent settings from session-only overrides.
3. Open Terrain tools and exercise all eight proposal operations Raise/Lower/Flatten/Ramp/Slope/Pad/Cut/Fill.
   Record radius, target/delta applicability, material and footprint for each; Add, select and delete-cancel/confirm
   must affect the exact proposal ID. Palette circles at origin are proposals, not proof of committed deformation.
   Preview/heatmap must not write DB data or bump committed terrain revision; use before/after API payloads.

   sectors. Require durable region nodes and visible effects; weighted radial strokes differ from constant-depth
   defined shapes. Exercise Brush/Circle/Rectangle/Polygon selection, Apply/clear and insufficient polygon state.
   Current incomplete Polygon producer remains a failed/blocked reachable feature, never a passing empty draft.
