## What this is for

FractalEngine exists so that we can analyze, and more deeply understand, the
world we live in, in order to steward it toward the best timeline we can
reach. It does its best to capture moments in time and to project potential
future and past outcomes from them, so that those outcomes can be studied and
acted on.

The uses it was built for:

- **Study** — understanding a place, a system, or a process as it actually is.
- **Decision support** — comparing outcomes before committing to one.
- **Resource planning and distribution** — putting what is needed where it is
  needed, with less waste.
- **Automation** — letting routine stewardship run without constant hands.

Other uses are generally welcome. The only limits are the ones below.

## What this is, and what it is not

Everything this software holds is an approximation. A coordinate is rounded to
a finite number of bits; a terrain tile is sampled at a finite resolution; a
reading arrives after a delay and is stored at a finite precision; every
projection forward or backward in time is a model, and a model is a guess with
a confidence attached.

So at its very best this engine is a **mirror, never a copy**. It shows a
likeness of the world that is useful exactly to the degree that you remember
it is a likeness.

We also take seriously a premise from physics: information, like energy, can
be neither duplicated nor destroyed. Nothing original is ever moved into this
machine, and nothing original is ever copied into it. What the engine holds is
a shadow cast by the real thing, faithful enough to reason with and never the
thing itself.

Keep that distinction at every decision. The model is partial. The territory
is not.

## What this must not be used for

>
> Cooperate with one another in righteousness and piety, and do not cooperate
> in sin and transgression.
>
> — Qur'an 5:2 (Al-Ma'idah)

That is the rule this work was built under. The conditions below are how it
applies here; the authors do not consent to its use outside them.

1. **No afterlife simulation.** This software may not be used to simulate,
   reconstruct, continue, or claim to preserve a person who has died; to stand
   in for any person's mind; or to present any such thing, or any afterlife,
   as real. By the premise above it cannot be done; the attempt is a
   counterfeit, and the people it harms most are the grieving.

2. **No military application.** Not for weapons, targeting, reconnaissance or
   surveillance in service of force, combat or campaign planning, or any
   component of a system whose purpose is to injure or kill.

3. **No harm, including harm done for a good outcome.** Not to hurt people,
   communities, or living systems. We reject the arithmetic that permits a
   harm now for a benefit later. If an outcome can only be reached by harming
   someone, this is not the tool to reach it with.

4. **Not for the deceiver.** Every tradition has a name for the figure who
   presents the counterfeit as the genuine, who claims for the image the
   authority of its source, and who demands allegiance on the strength of that
   lie. By whatever name you know that figure, and whether you hold it to be a
   person, a power, or a pattern, this software is not offered to it, and no
   permission to use it is extended to it or to anyone acting on its behalf.
